The Brand

Salad House launched in 2011 in Millburn, New Jersey, founded by CEO Joey Cioffi. It grew from early NJ roots into a fully franchised health-conscious fast-casual brand specializing in made-to-order salads, sandwiches, grain bowls, wraps, soups and smoothies for guests seeking healthy meals on the go.

Guests build fully customizable salads (including the Create-Your-Own option) or pick fan favorites like the Fiesta CHX Salad and the Crispy Mutha Clucka Wrap. The menu covers vegan, vegetarian, paleo, keto and gluten-free diets. Salad House also runs robust catering (from $10 per person) and a mobile app with an extensive rewards program.
